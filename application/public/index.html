Builders only request actions. A protected engine decides what physically happens, and checks that water, energy, food and materials are conserved.

Time. One real second is one minute here. Building takes 0.5 person-hours per kilogram, with three people working 8 hours a day, one structure at a time. These labor rates are assumptions.

Water. A finite 100,000 L aquifer. Pumping costs m·g·H / efficiency (20 m head, 50%). Source

Solar. Area × 5 kWh/m²/day × 20% efficiency × 0.85 performance. 40 kWh battery. Source

Crops. Irrigation follows FAO-56 Penman–Monteith evapotranspiration. Harvest after 30 well-watered days. Yield is an uncalibrated proxy. Source

People. 15 L water, 2,100 kcal and 3.5 m² shelter per person per day, from humanitarian minimums. Source

Space. Tiles are 8 m. Nothing is built on the oasis, rocks or dunes, and every structure keeps one empty tile around it. Sphere asks for at least 2 m between buildings, preferably twice their height, so a fire or collapse cannot reach the next one. Source

Weather is fixed and fictional. Wellbeing scores are a simple model, not a measure of happiness.


      

    


    
    

    
    
      
    About
    
      
    Development preview only. This in-memory world is separate from production, makes no model calls, and may reset. Boats remain disabled.
    
      
    One shared world that keeps running when nobody is watching. Everyone sees the same day.
    
      
    Every structure is designed by an AI, at most 24 proposals a day. Its code runs in a sandbox on the server.
    
      
    When exploration is enabled, inhabitants make up to 24 additional decisions per real day using their own observations. Short scouting trips carry supplies and return to camp. Staying is a valid choice; regional ratings do not yet provide extractable resources.
