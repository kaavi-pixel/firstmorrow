Live

This is reality. It is not the AI's to change. The people live inside it; the AI writes code that works within it.

Matter is finite. Water, soil and material are drawn from a tile and never return. Nothing comes from nothing: every process is mass-checked, and a program's output mass may never exceed what it took in.

Bodies. 15 L of water and 2,100 kcal per person per day; 20 kg carried. Miss either and health falls, and at zero they die for good.

Work. 8 person-hours per person per day. Construction costs 0.9 person-hours per kg — the kernel computes that; a program cannot claim a shorter build.

Processes. A finished structure runs its author's code: at most 5 kg per labour hour. A garden yields 0.35 kg of food per kg of soil. A still beside the sea yields 4 L of fresh water per labour hour. These are game assumptions, stated plainly, not physics.

Weather is real and it destroys. A storm wrecks what was not built to stand, and takes the unfinished work with it. Anyone caught in the open loses health.

Life and death. People age and die of it. They can conceive and bear children — but only while they are healthy and there is food. There is no rescue, no repair, and no bringing back the dead.
